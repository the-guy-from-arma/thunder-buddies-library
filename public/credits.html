A letter to the community ↓

TO THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE PROJECTS

Keep building.
Even when I’m no longer
building beside you.

To Guys That Think They Know Enfusion, and to the wider Arma community: thank you.

The files in this library are the part of this chapter I can leave behind. They cannot capture everything that made it matter: the conversations around an idea, the patience it takes to learn, or the feeling of finally getting something to work and wanting to show someone who understands.

I am stepping away from my chapter in Arma. That is hard to put into a sentence. There is work here I am proud of, work I would approach differently now, and unfinished ideas that someone else may see a future in. I want to leave room for all of it.

Please keep building without me.

Open the projects. Ask the questions. Fix what is broken. Take an idea somewhere I never thought to take it. Help the next person who is staring at an error they do not understand. Make space for people who are still learning.

You do not have to preserve my work exactly as I left it. Give it a future. Keep the credit with it, and add your own chapter.

This thank-you names the community because I do not want the people to disappear behind the files. Whether you shared something, asked a question, followed along quietly, or are only just finding your place here: there is room for you in what comes next.

Thank you for being part of the Arma chapter of Thunder Buddies. I hope these files give something back.

I’m leaving this chapter in your hands.

Keep building. Look after one another.
Make something worth sharing.

— Thunder Buddies Studios
